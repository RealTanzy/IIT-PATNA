Background

A* Search vs Chain-of-Thought — How They Work

🔗 Chain-of-Thought (CoT)

Linear reasoning — one step at a time, no backtracking.

Q → Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3 → Answer

✅ Fast, fluent, works on single-step tasks
❌ Commits early, cannot backtrack on errors
Temp τ=0.7, SC uses 5 samples at τ=0.9

🌳 A* Search

Graph-based — explores multiple branches guided by f(n)=g(n)+h(n).

Q → {B1,B2,B3} → score → expand best → ... → Answer

✅ Systematic multi-hop, backtracking
❌ Higher compute, can overthink simple Qs
Nc=3, B=15, critic τ=0.3

⚖️ Why A* is Theoretically Sound

Admissibility verified empirically on 500 annotated states:

  • Case 2 satisfied: critic mean score 0.43 vs human 0.58 (p<0.001) → conservative, admissible in expectation
  • Case 3 satisfied: overestimation rate 12.3%, ε_max=0.14 → bounded sub-optimality
  • Sufficient but not necessary — search budget B acts as regulariser

Methodology

Topic Modelling — How We Found the 8 Categories

1

Pool all 2,151 questions (dataset labels ignored)

LogicQA (651) + StrategyQA (500) + HotpotQA (1,000). Cross-dataset mixing is intentional — we want topics that cut across benchmarks, not within them.

2

TF-IDF Vectorisation

5,000 features, max_df=0.80, min_df=3, ngram_range=(1,2). Bigrams like "multi hop", "time period" captured. Common words down-weighted. Produces a 2151 × 5000 matrix.

3

NMF — Non-negative Matrix Factorisation

Decomposes 2151×5000 into k topic components. Non-negativity constraint → additive, parts-based, interpretable topics. Each question assigned to its highest-scoring topic.

4

Select k=8 via NPMI Coherence (Mimno et al. 2011)

Swept k∈{6,8,10}. k=8 peaks at NPMI=0.127. Bootstrap stability: 87.3% topic-assignment agreement over 10 folds (std 2.1%). Finding robust: A*-dominant at 79.2% (k=6) and 82.1% (k=10).

5

Measure A* vs CoT accuracy per topic on balanced data

Same model, same questions, same evaluation. Whoever wins majority of per-dataset comparisons is the topic winner.

NPMI Coherence vs k — Why k=8

Why NPMI over Perplexity?

Perplexity always improves as k increases — never gives a peak. NPMI measures semantic coherence: "do top words of this topic co-occur more than chance?" This correlates with human interpretability (Mimno et al. 2011, EMNLP).

Overthinking is structural — A*'s thoroughness is a liability on single-step problems. SLM amplification: A* substitutes the working memory Qwen lacks. On LogicQA, both model sizes lose with A* — topology-driven, not capability-driven.

📉 Efficiency Trade-off — When Is A* Worth It?

A* uses ~972 tokens/question vs CoT's 200. But on StrategyQA, A* reaches 74.80% while CoT saturates at 64.52% — a gain CoT cannot achieve at any token budget. SC uses ~600 tokens and still achieves lower accuracy than A* on branching tasks, confirming guided search > unguided sampling.

🔍 Qualitative Examples

A* wins: "Is shrimp scampi free of plastic?" — CoT concludes YES; A* finds microplastic bioaccumulation in Step 1, absence of removal in Step 2 → NO (correct).

CoT wins: "Is Boeing 737 cost covered by Wonder Woman box office?" — CoT retrieves magnitudes directly. A* misreads it as a financial transactions query — overthinking.

✅ What Works

  • A* on multi-hop: content, assumption, temporal, biography
  • Pre-generation structural routing > post-hoc trace inspection
  • Search amplifies SLMs where working memory is the bottleneck
  • BC alone explains 94% of variance in A* advantage (R²=0.94)
  • Two independent lenses (topology + topics) converge on same conclusion

❌ What Doesn't Work

  • A* on single-step tasks (comparison, statement strength, option selection)
  • Fluency as a routing signal — 38% systematic misrouting ceiling
  • Aggregate benchmarks — they conceal structural patterns completely
  • Topology router still leaves 73.9% oracle gap unclosed

🚀 Next Steps

  • Close 73.9% residual: problem–model compatibility scoring
  • Extend to reasoning-native models (o1-mini, DeepSeek-R1)
  • Learn topology embeddings instead of hand-crafted features
  • Calibrated uncertainty-aware search budgeting
  • Multi-language topology transfer
Central message: Neither A* nor CoT universally dominates. Route by topology — pre-generation, using only text structure, before any inference begins.
