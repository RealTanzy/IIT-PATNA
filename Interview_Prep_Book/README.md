## What This Book Is

This is not a textbook. This is a battle manual.

Every concept in this book was used in a real system — deployed at Mercedes-Benz, submitted to a top ML journal, or shipped to production users. There are no hypothetical examples. When I explain Bayesian Log-Likelihood Ratios, I show you the exact engine that achieved 97% accuracy on failure analysis. When I explain A* search, I walk you through the admissibility proof I published.

If you're preparing for AI/ML interviews — whether at Anthropic, Google, a startup, or a research lab — this book covers everything from the fundamentals to the systems to the 200+ questions they'll actually ask you.

## How To Use This Book

**If you have an interview in 2 days:** Start with Chapter 16. Read the questions for the role you're targeting.

**If you have a week:** Read Chapters 6-9 (systems) and 16. These cover what 80% of interviews ask about.

**If you're studying systematically:** Go cover to cover. Every chapter builds on the previous one.

**If you're asked "tell me about a project":** Chapters 6-11 each have a full case study with STAR-format answers.
