03
Reviewer 1 · 9TPm

Borderline Accept

Overall 2.5 Soundness 3 Confidence 4
Concern
Routing gap between oracle and actual router is large — is this a fundamental flaw in the work?
Our Response
It's a finding, not a flaw. We quantified the Fluency-Correctness Asymmetry: 38% of divergent cases had fluent-but-wrong CoT traces — a hard ceiling for any output-based router.
Concern
Heuristic may be dataset-specific and not generalise across domains.
Our Response
Validated on 500 annotated states across all 3 datasets. Max overestimation ε = 0.14 was consistent — not dataset-dependent.
Concern
No sensitivity analysis on search budget B or branching factor K.
Our Response
Budget sweep B∈{5..30}: peaked at B=20 (+5.7pp over B=5). Branching sweep K∈{1..4}: K=4 hit 80.3%, even K=1 gave 77.7% — robust across all settings.
04
⚡ Reviewer 2 · Gftu — Harshest

Resubmit

Overall 1.5 Soundness 2.5 Confidence 4
Concern
Models too small (8B / 0.5B) — conclusions may not hold at scale.
Our Response
Claim is structural, not absolute. LogicQA resists search at both model sizes. HotpotQA gain 3.5× on smaller model confirms capability amplification — a scale-independent finding.
Concern
Router uses handcrafted features — not portable or scalable.
Our Response
Topology router uses 13 pre-generation features, zero LLM calls, and recovers 26.1% of oracle gap — beating DeepSeek-as-critic that reads full traces.
Concern
No compute / token cost analysis against simpler baselines.
Our Response
A* K=1 (3,372 tok/q) already beats SC at 77.7% vs 78.2%. StrategyQA +10.3pp is unreachable by CoT sampling alone — SC peaks at 71.2%, still 3.6pp below A*.

✨ Reviewer 3 · tgwN

Borderline · High Excitement

Overall 2.5 Excitement 3.5 ✨ Confidence 3
Concern
LLM judge is DeepSeek — results may be entangled with that specific model.
Our Response
BFS ablation (DeepSeek never consulted): 77.0%. Greedy: 77.7%. Full A*: 76.0%. All within 1.7pp — the judge is not load-bearing. Branching structure drives the gain.
Concern
Only one heuristic — performance may be heuristic-dependent.
Our Response
Framework: h=0 → BFS; g=0 → Greedy; full → A*. Ablation shows all variants perform similarly — heuristic choice is not the driver.
Concern
Only QA benchmarks — unclear if findings generalise to math or code.
Our Response
13 topology features are text-level and domain-agnostic. Framework predicts search helps on multi-step math — confirmed by rStar-Math 2025.
